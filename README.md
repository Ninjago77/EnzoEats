# EnzoEats
A FPS shooter-esque multiplayer meele & ranged food fight game, where you just pummel your friends with random foods you find on the floor.
Download at:- https://ninjago77.itch.io/enzoeats or https://github.com/Ninjago77/EnzoEats/releases/tag/v0.1.0
## Controls
WASD: Movement
Shift: Sprint
Space: Jump
E: PickUp
Q: Drop
Left Click: Fire/Throw
## How to Connect
Open the Game/Exe (After Extracting the Zip File), type in your Nickname & a room code (tell your friends to type in the same room code), then click join!
